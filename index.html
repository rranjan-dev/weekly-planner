Key Principles for a Productive Routine
Weekdays = Maintenance, Weekends = Growth
Weekday cognitive budget is spent on office work. Don't try to learn new DSA patterns at 6 PM after 8 hours of work — revise what you know. Save deep learning for fresh weekend mornings.
Protect the Morning Block (4:30–7:15)
All spiritual + exercise done before the world demands your attention. This is your "sacred time" — no phone, no distractions. If this goes well, the rest of the day follows. If you skip this, everything cascades.
Sleep is Non-Negotiable
6.5 hours is your minimum. If you sleep at 10:30 instead of 10:00, wake at 4:45 — don't sacrifice sleep. A tired mind will make your 16 waking hours inefficient.
Cluster Calls Together
Family + wife calls back-to-back avoids context switching. Don't scatter them across the evening — batch relationships time like you batch work.
Relaxation is Intentional, Not Leftover
30 minutes of guilt-free entertainment is scheduled, not squeezed in. When it's on the calendar, you enjoy it. When it's stolen time, you feel guilty and it doesn't recharge you.
Limit YouTube Shorts to 30 Minutes
Shorts are designed to be addictive. Set a timer. This is your Q4 (Not Urgent, Not Important) — the Eisenhower Matrix says eliminate or strictly limit. Don't let 30 minutes become 2 hours.
The 2-Minute Rule for Transitions
Between activities, take 2 minutes to close the previous context mentally. Don't carry office stress into family calls, or DSA frustration into relaxation time. A clean mental break between blocks makes each block more effective.
Weekly, Not Daily, Perfection
You will miss a day. That's fine. Aim for 6/7 days on your non-negotiables. The streak mentality helps, but don't let one bad day spiral into "I've already failed this week."
Consistency Over Intensity
The key differentiator between people who sustain this and those who burn out: it's better to do 80% of this schedule for 6 months than 100% for 2 weeks and then collapse. Start with this plan, track for 2 weeks, then adjust based on what you learn about your energy patterns.
